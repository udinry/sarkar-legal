Connecting a dashboard

  1. Open the app's dashboard settings.
  2. Enter the URL of your owner dashboard server and its password.
  3. Allow notifications when the app asks, so alerts can reach your device.

Importing an App Store Connect key

  1. Create an App Store Connect API key in App Store Connect.
  2. In the app, import that key.
  3. The key is stored in your iPhone's Keychain.

Common questions

Push notifications are not arriving. Check that notifications are allowed for Sarkar in iPhone Settings → Notifications, and that your dashboard is connected in the app.

Weather is missing from my briefing. Allow location access for Sarkar in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Weather uses your approximate location.

Is my data uploaded anywhere? The developer collects no data. Data you create stays on your device. See the privacy policy.

How do I export or delete my data? Settings → Export everything and Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app removes everything.

Does Sarkar AI cost me anything? No. It's included; usage is capped on our side.
