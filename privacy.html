Sarkar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Sarkar is made by Udbhav Naryani.

The short version: the developer collects no data. Data you create stays on your device, and the app connects only to services you choose.

No data collection by the developer

The developer collects no data. There are no accounts with us, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

Data on your device

Data you create in Sarkar (tasks, habits, subscriptions, focus sessions, vault items, scans and uptime monitors) stays on your device. Vault items are encrypted on the device.

Services you choose

The app connects only to the following, and only when you use them:

  • Your owner dashboard server. You enter its URL and password. It shows your stats and alerts. To deliver push notifications, the dashboard stores your device's push token, and notifications are sent through Apple Push Notification service.
  • App Store Connect. With the API key you import, the app shows your apps, reviews and sales. The key is stored in your device's Keychain.
  • Apple WeatherKit. Your approximate location is used to get the weather for your briefing.
  • Websites you add to Uptime monitoring.

Calendar, reminders and Health

Calendar, reminders and Health data are read on the device only. They are never sent anywhere.

On-device AI

Where available, on-device AI features run fully on the device.

Sarkar AI (optional)

Sarkar AI is off until you turn it on in Settings → Sarkar AI and agree to the consent screen. You can turn it off any time, and then nothing is sent.

When Sarkar AI is on and you tap an AI feature, or talk to Sarkar, the app sends only the data that feature needs through our relay server to Anthropic's Claude to produce the answer. For example:

  • your channel numbers for a weekly report;
  • the text of an alert for "Explain";
  • your typed or spoken request for "Talk to Sarkar".

Our relay checks that you are signed in to your own dashboard and counts usage for a monthly spending cap. It does not store or log the content of requests or answers. Anthropic processes the request under its API terms.

Vault items are never sent. Voice is transcribed on your device, and only the resulting text is sent.

On-device AI (Apple Intelligence) features run entirely on the device.

Your data, your control

Export everything with Settings → Export everything. Delete all data with Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app removes everything.

Children

Sarkar is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: dev@udbhav.uk.
